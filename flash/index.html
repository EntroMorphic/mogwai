mogwai

Natural-language control for ESP32-class hardware. Integer-only nearest-neighbour routing — no model, no floats, no training. Routes an utterance in 4.3 ms with the whole index resident in SRAM.

Plug in an ESP32 over USB and click. Nothing to install; this uses WebSerial, so it needs Chrome or Edge.

This erases the board. If it shipped with ESP-AT or anything else you want back, save it first — see board_backup/RESTORE.md.

Requirements

  • 4 MB of flash or more. The partition table puts a 2 MB app at 0x10000, so the image needs 0x210000 bytes and the bootloader is built for a 4 MB layout. Most ESP32 devkits are 4 MB.
  • An ESP32 (LX6). Not S2/S3/C3/C6 — the popcount table and DRAM placement are specific to this part.
  • Chrome or Edge, for WebSerial.

Then

Open a serial monitor at 115200 and type an utterance:

> turn the lights on
  iot_hue_lighton score 227  (margin +91)
  ACTUATED     light -> ON  (duty 255/255)
  4437 us

Default pins: light PWM on GPIO2 (the onboard LED on most devkits), wemo GPIO4, cleaning GPIO16, coffee GPIO17. GPIO16 and 17 are the PSRAM lines on WROVER modules — override them at build time if you have one.

What it does not do

It routes nine IoT intents from a public dataset and rejects everything else. On held-out data it actuates wrongly on 12 of 2754 non-commands (0.44%) — read FRAME.md for the scope and limits behind every number.
